Two detectors, one signal

Hanford, WashingtonLivingston, Louisiana

Hear the one-second extract at its recorded speed. Use headphones to compare Hanford on the left and adjusted Livingston on the right. Each channel uses the same playback gain.

Reading the traces

Livingston received the wave about seven milliseconds before Hanford. Moving Livingston later and reversing its sign accounts approximately for travel time and the detectors’ different orientations. Noise remains different at each site. The visual overlap and the correlation score respond to your choices.

Each 32-second strain record is whitened using its own noise spectrum and band-pass filtered from 35 to 350 Hz. Values are in units of that detector’s filtered off-event noise. Time zero is a display reference at GPS 1126259462.4.

The LIGO Scientific Collaboration, Virgo Collaboration and KAGRA provide the original strain through GWOSC. Chirp Room filters and crops the recordings for this comparison. CC BY 4.0 data licence · GWTC-1 release DOI · GWOSC acknowledgements.
